The memory route

The whole course as one walk down Smart Boulevard: 21 places in three districts, one idea per place. Each place holds a strange, physical scene, and the strangeness is what makes it stick. Walk it once a day for a week and you can recall the course without notes.

Click a stop

Or press "Walk the route". In "Test me" mode the idea is hidden: picture the scene, say the idea out loud, then reveal.

How to use the route

  1. Walk it slowly once, reading every scene and picturing it in colour, with sound and smell.
  2. Walk it from memory: close your eyes, go from place to place and say each idea.
  3. Test me mode each day for a week; spend extra time on the stops you miss.
  4. Add your own details: numbers you need for an exam can be painted onto objects in the scene.
